Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This site is a demo. It isn't run by a real cinema, tickets aren't valid for real screenings, and payments run in test mode, so no real money is ever charged.

Cinefy is an open-source, non-profit cinema management and booking project. This policy explains what information the Cinefy booking site collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your first name, last name, email address and phone number. If you sign up with a password, it's stored only as a secure hash and can't be read by anyone.
  • Information from OAuth providers: if you sign in with an OAuth provider, we receive your name and email address, and use them only to create your account and sign you in. We don't receive your password or any other data from that account.
  • Booking information: the showtimes and seats you book, your booking reference, your ticket's QR code and the payment status of each booking.
  • Saved cards: if you choose to save a card, our payment provider gives us a token, the card brand and its last digits. We never receive or store your full card number or security code.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our servers may log technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. These logs are used only for security and troubleshooting.

How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To process your bookings and payments and issue your tickets.
  • To send you emails you need: verification codes, password reset codes and your tickets. We don't send marketing emails or newsletters.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or build profiles about you.

Cookies

Cinefy uses only the cookies the site needs to work: to keep you signed in, to protect your requests against forgery, and to secure the sign-in process with OAuth providers. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party services

Cinefy relies on these services to work:

  • Paymob processes card payments. You enter your card details on Paymob's own checkout page, not on Cinefy.
  • OAuth providers handle sign-in when you use them.
  • Gmail delivers the emails we send you.
  • TMDB provides movie information. Movie posters and images load from TMDB's servers, but TMDB receives none of your account information.
  • YouTube hosts movie trailers. When you play a trailer, YouTube may collect information under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on this site.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy, such as Google's and TMDB's.

How we protect your information

Information is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and payment provider credentials are encrypted. Only authorized cinema staff can access the management dashboard. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it along with its personal information.

Your choices

  • You can view and update your name and phone number, change your password and remove saved cards from your profile.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, or for your account to be deleted, by emailing mdevs.lab@gmail.com from the address on your account. We'll confirm once your request is complete.

Open source

Cinefy's source code is public, so you can see exactly how your information is handled on GitHub.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or your information, email mdevs.lab@gmail.com.
